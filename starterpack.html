Getting started

Your first two weeks

  1. Welcome call with your line managerIn your first few days, Zahra, Hamid or Dean walks you through how Nourish works and your objectives.
  2. Intro callsA series of short calls with the UX and Design team and the people you'll work with most closely, plus HR and Finance for the admin essentials.
  3. Set up your toolsAccept your Figma, Jira and Miro invites, and ask your line manager for GitHub access. You'll need it for Pulse and the Product Brain.
  4. Get to know the productWatch the sales demos and explore the demo environments. Both are under Tools and links.

Team days

We meet in person as a design team once a month, usually on the first Thursday. The location rotates in this order:

  • London (Paddington)
  • Oxford
  • Bristol
  • Bournemouth
  • Birmingham

Dates sometimes move, with at least 6 weeks' notice, so check Team dates on the noticeboard. If you're travelling far or staying overnight, you can claim travel and hotels. Book them through Perk, and ask HR or Finance if you're unsure.

121s

You'll have a fortnightly 121 with your line manager (Zahra, Hamid or Dean), a casual check-in to talk about how work is going and raise any concerns. Need something between 121s? Message them on Teams.

Working hours

Core hours are 9am to 5pm, Monday to Friday. We're flexible as long as you reply in good time, your product team's needs are covered and we can reach you when we need to.

Our team is supportive and passionate. We share work early, ask for help often and are encouraged to experiment. If you're ever unsure, stuck or overwhelmed, reach out. There's always someone happy to help.

Design system

Pulse

Pulse is Nourish's design system. Design with the Pulse libraries in Figma, and use the docs for how each component should be used.

Pulse docs Components, foundations and usage guidance. Sign in with GitHub.
PCOV board Pulse doesn't have what you need? Raise it here, and talk it through with your line manager.

AI-assisted design

Product Brain and Claude

The Product Brain holds Nourish's product context: strategy, personas, research and design work. We use it with Claude for research synthesis, journey maps and prototypes. You don't need it on day one. Pick it up once you're settled in.

The Brain is a private GitHub repository, so get GitHub access first and sign in. Then open the designers' guide, and ask your line manager to help you connect Claude.
