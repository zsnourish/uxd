New starter

Welcome to Nourish

Everything you need to settle in and find your feet. Take your first week or two to learn the ropes, meet people and get comfortable with the product.

Your first two weeks

Four things to do as you settle in. There's no rush: take them at your own pace.

  1. Welcome call with your line managerIn your first few days, Zahra, Hamid or Dean walks you through how Nourish works and your objectives.
  2. Intro callsA series of short calls with the UX and Design team and the people you'll work with most closely, plus HR and Finance for the admin essentials.
  3. Set up your toolsAccept your Figma, Jira and Miro invites. Then sign up to GitHub (opens in a new tab) with your Nourish email and ask Adam Hopkinson or Ben Galvin to add you to Nourish's GitHub organisation, nourishcare.
  4. Get to know the productWatch the sales demos and explore the demo environments. Both are in Tools and links.

Design ceremonies

Alongside your product team's meetings, these are the design team's regular touchpoints.

  • MondayUX StandupShare your top priorities for the week. Quick and focused.
  • ThursdaySpotlightTimed design crit. Bring work you're proud of, stuck on or want a second opinion on.
  • FridayDesign Show and TellShow the team what you've been working on.
  • QuarterlyTeam Health RetroAn anonymous vote on how things are going, with time to discuss and improve.

Team days

We meet in person once a month, usually on the first Thursday. The location rotates in this order:

  1. London (Paddington)
  2. Oxford
  3. Bristol
  4. Bournemouth
  5. Birmingham

Dates sometimes move, with at least 6 weeks' notice, so check Team dates (opens in a new tab). You can claim travel and hotels for long trips. Book them through Perk, and ask HR or Finance if you're unsure.

121s

A fortnightly 121 with your line manager (Zahra, Hamid or Dean): a casual check-in on how work is going and anything on your mind. Need something in between? Message them on Teams.

Working hours

Core hours are 9am to 5pm, Monday to Friday. We're flexible as long as you reply in good time, your product team's needs are covered and we can reach you when we need to.

We share work early, ask for help often and are encouraged to experiment. If you're ever unsure, stuck or overwhelmed, reach out. There's always someone happy to help.

Next: Pulse

Pulse

Pulse is Nourish's design system: the components, styles and guidance every product team designs and builds with. It keeps our products consistent and accessible, and means nobody designs the same thing twice.

How you'll use it

  1. Design with the librariesAdd Pulse Components and Pulse Foundations from the Assets panel in Figma, and build your screens from them.
  2. Check the guidanceThe Pulse docs explain when to use each component, with live examples.
  3. Raise a gapIf Pulse doesn't have what you need, raise it on the PCOV board and talk it through with your line manager.

Pulse links

  • Pulse ComponentsThe component library in Figma
  • Pulse FoundationsColour, type and icons in Figma
  • Pulse docsUsage guidance and live examples
  • PCOV boardWhere Pulse gaps are raised and tracked

You sign in to the Pulse docs with GitHub. If you haven't yet, sign up to GitHub (opens in a new tab) with your Nourish email and ask Adam Hopkinson or Ben Galvin to add you to the nourishcare organisation.

Next: Product Brain

Product Brain and Claude

The Product Brain holds Nourish's product context: strategy, bets, personas, research and design work. Used with Claude, it's the quickest way to get up to speed on your product area, and it speeds up discovery and design work.

What you can do with it

  • Get up to speedAsk about your product area, its bets, customers and personas
  • Make sense of researchTurn research into empathy maps and How Might We questions
  • Map journeysCurrent and future journeys, and service blueprints
  • PrototypeBuild clickable prototypes to test and share ideas

Get set up

  1. Get GitHub accessThe Brain is the private repository nourishcare/nourish-product-brain. Sign up to GitHub (opens in a new tab) with your Nourish email, then ask Adam Hopkinson or Ben Galvin to add you to the nourishcare organisation. Send them your GitHub username. Until you're added, the Brain's links show a GitHub 404 page.
  2. Read the designers' guideOnce you're signed in to GitHub, start with the guide for designers (opens in a new tab).
  3. Connect ClaudeYour line manager will help you connect Claude to the Brain, so you can start using it straight away.
